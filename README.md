Palm Shores is an offline cover art studio that turns any photo and caption into multi-panel GTA 6-style poster art with a Vice City sunset or neon grade, and exports it as a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Photos are cropped and positioned per panel, stay on your machine, and the app needs no network.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
